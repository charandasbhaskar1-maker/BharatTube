BharatTube Partner Program (Monetization Niyam)

💰 BharatTube Kamai Niyamavali

BharatTube apne Bharatiya creators ko unke original content se kamai karne ka poora adhikar deta hai. Agar aap hamare niyam aur sharto ka palan karte hain, toh aap seedhe apne bank khate ya UPI ke madhyam se paise kama sakte hain.

🎯 Yogyata Shartein (Eligibility Criteria)

BharatTube ke prathmik daur (Initial Phase) mein kamai shuru karne ke liye creator ko yeh do target poore karne honge:

200
Subscribers Zaroori
100 Ghante
Public Watch Time
  • Aapke channel par 0 Active Copyright Strikes hone chahiye.
  • Channel ne Community Guidelines ka koi ghanaghor ullanghan na kiya ho.
  • Watch time keval public videos ka hi gina jayega (private ya unlisted ka nahi).

📈 Revenue Share (Kamai ka Batwara)

BharatTube par kamai ka hissa YouTube ki tarah transparent hai:

  • 55% Kamai Creator ko: Video par chalne wale ads aur Super Thanks ka 55% hissa seedhe creator ko milta hai.
  • 45% Platform Maintenance: Servers, video streaming infrastructure aur payment gateway ke liye.

⚡ Payouts aur Payment Gateways

Aapki har mahine ki kamai direct UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) ya NEFT/Bank Account ke madhyam se har mahine ki 21 se 26 tarikh ke beech bhej di jati hai.

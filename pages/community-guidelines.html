BharatTube Community Disha-Nirdesh

🛡️ Surakshit aur Vishwasniya Platform

BharatTube ka lakshya har Bharatiya nagrik aur parivar ke liye ek surakshit manoranjan aur shiksha ka manch pradan karna hai. Sabhi creators aur darshakon ko in niyamon ka palan karna anivarya hai.

🚫 Kaunsi Videos BharatTube Par Ban Hain?

  • Spam aur Dhokhadhadi: Galat title, clickbait thumbnail, ya jhoothi scheme/lottery wali videos.
  • Ashlil ya Adult Content: Nudity, pornography ya kisi bhi prakar ka ashleel material sakhti se pratibandhit hai.
  • Hinsa aur Nafrat (Hate Speech): Kisi jati, dharam, ling ya samuday ke khilaf bhedbhav ya hinsa bhadkane wala content.
  • Bachhon ki Suraksha: Bachhon ke mansik ya sharirik shoshan se judi koi bhi samagri platform se turant hata di jayegi.
  • Desh-Virodhi ya Khaufnak Samagri: Rashtriya suraksha ko nuksan pahunchane wali ya aatankwad ko badhava dene wali samagri.

⚠️ Strike System (Niyam Todne Par Karyavahi)

Agar koi creator in disha-nirdeshon ka ullanghan karta hai, toh BharatTube par teen strikes ka niyam lagu hota hai:

Pehli Bar (Warning): Video ko platform se hata diya jayega aur channel ko ek formal chetavni di jayegi.
Strike 1: 1 hafte tak video upload, live streaming aur post karne par rok.
Strike 2: 2 hafte tak poori creator activities par pratibandh.
Strike 3: Channel ko permanently BharatTube se terminate kar diya jayega aur monetization hamesha ke liye band ho jayega.

©️ BharatTube Copyright Niti

BharatTube par keval vahi content upload karein jisko aapne swayam banaya hai ya jiske uplabdh hone ka aapke paas legal adhikar hai. Doosron ki video, audio ya movie clips bina anumati ke chori karna gair-kanooni hai.

🔍 BharatTube Content ID System

Humara automated system har nayi uploaded video ko scan karta hai. Agar kisi video mein copyright wala gana ya clip milti hai:

  • Asli malik chahe toh video par ads chala kar khud kamai kar sakta hai.
  • Asli malik video ko BharatTube se turant block kar sakta hai.

⚖️ Fair Use (Uchit Upyog)

Aap alochana, shiksha, samachar ya commentary ke liye kisi doosre ke content ka chhota hissa upyog kar sakte hain, basharte aapne usme apni original voiceover ya review joda ho.

🚨 Copyright Takedown Notice

Agar kisi copyright malik ne aapki video ke khilaf formal legal takedown bheja, toh video hata di jayegi aur aapko 1 Copyright Strike milegi. Agar aapko lagta hai ki notice galti se aaya hai, toh aap Counter-Notification file kar sakte hain.
